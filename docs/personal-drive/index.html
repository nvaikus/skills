Personal Drive

Personal Drive is an open-source command-line tool that connects your own Google Drive to your own computer: it mounts Drive as a local folder, keeps a local text index of your files for search, and reads or edits your Google Docs and Sheets on your request.

Every user runs it under their own Google Cloud project and their own OAuth client. There is no server: nothing is sent to anyone except Google.
